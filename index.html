About Our Factory

Based in Hasilpur, we are a family-owned business committed to quality.

JS Silage & Wanda Factory has been at the heart of Pakistan's agricultural community for over a decade. Our state-of-the-art facility produces premium silage in Open, Baled, and Bags to meet the diverse needs of livestock farmers nationwide.

In addition, we proudly offer Wanda — a specialized feed supplement designed for enhanced animal nutrition and health.
